# Before and After: An RFI

Illustrative. Fictional project and details.

---

## The note that came off the field

> *"Cant fit the brace at B3 level 2, gusset hits the HSS column, need direction
> asap, holding 4 pieces"*

Real, and unusable. Sent as-is it produces a phone call, a site visit, and three
days.

## What went into the template

```
THE CONDITION: Vertical brace connection at grid B3, Level 2. The gusset plate
shown on S-301 Rev 2 detail 4 conflicts with the HSS8x8x1/2 column at the
work point. As detailed, the gusset would need to pass through the column face.

DRAWINGS: S-301 Rev 2 (detail 4), S-201 Rev 3 (framing plan, Level 2),
S-102 Rev 1 (brace elevation)

WHAT I HAVE ALREADY CHECKED: S-301 Rev 2 detail 4 and its section cut;
S-201 Rev 3 for member sizes at B3; S-102 Rev 1 brace geometry; spec section
05 12 00 for connection design responsibility (connections are prescribed on the
drawings, not delegated); no prior RFI on this connection.

SCHEDULE IMPACT: 4 brace assemblies held at fabrication as of 2026-05-11.
Sequence 2 release-to-fab is 2026-05-22.

MY PROPOSED RESOLUTION: None — the geometry appears to require a design change.
```

## What came back

> **SUBJECT:** Gusset plate conflict at vertical brace connection, Grid B3,
> Level 2 — S-301 Rev 2 Detail 4
>
> **QUESTION:** Detail 4 on S-301 Rev 2 locates the brace gusset plate at a work
> point that coincides with the face of the HSS8x8x1/2 column shown on S-201
> Rev 3. As detailed, the gusset cannot be installed without passing through the
> column. Please direct the intended connection configuration at this location.
>
> **BACKGROUND:** S-301 Rev 2, Detail 4 shows the vertical brace gusset
> connection at braced frame locations. S-201 Rev 3 identifies the Level 2
> column at Grid B3 as HSS8x8x1/2. S-102 Rev 1 establishes brace geometry and
> work point. Applying the detail at this geometry places the gusset within the
> column footprint.
>
> We have reviewed S-301 Rev 2, S-201 Rev 3, S-102 Rev 1, and specification
> section 05 12 00. Section 05 12 00 prescribes connections on the structural
> drawings and does not delegate connection design to the fabricator. No prior
> RFI addresses this location.
>
> **IMPACT:** Four brace assemblies for Sequence 2 are held at fabrication as of
> 2026-05-11. Release to fabrication for Sequence 2 is scheduled 2026-05-22. A
> response by 2026-05-18 avoids impact to that date.

## What changed

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Describes confusion | Asks one answerable question |
| No references | Every claim tied to a sheet and revision |
| "asap" | A date, and what it protects |
| Invites a phone call | Can be answered in writing |

## The part that mattered most

Stating that spec section 05 12 00 *prescribes* connections rather than
delegating them. It preempts the most likely deflection — "the fabricator's
engineer should resolve this" — by putting the responsibility question on the
record before it is asked.

Note also what the RFI does not do: it proposes nothing. The field note had no
proposed fix, so the template omitted the section rather than inventing one. Had
there been a proposal, it would have gone in marked *for the engineer's review
and approval* — never as a decision.
