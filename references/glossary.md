# Glossary

Terms as used across this library.

**AISC** — American Institute of Steel Construction. Publishes the Steel
Construction Manual, the Specification for Structural Steel Buildings, and the
Code of Standard Practice.

**ASI** — Architect's Supplemental Instruction. A clarification or minor change
issued by the architect.

**Code of Standard Practice** — AISC document establishing default trade
practice, including division of work. Frequently modified by contract.

**Competent person** — someone capable of identifying hazards and authorized to
correct them. A regulatory term with legal weight, not a compliment.

**COR / CO** — Change Order Request; Change Order once executed.

**Delegated connection design** — connections designed by the fabricator's
engineer to criteria set by the EOR, rather than detailed on the structural
drawings.

**EOR** — Engineer of Record.

**Entitlement** — the contractual right to additional money or time, as distinct
from the amount. Established by the delta from a documented baseline.

**Erection tolerance** — permissible deviation of erected steel from theoretical
position. Governed by the Code of Standard Practice and the contract.

**JHA / JSA** — Job Hazard Analysis; Job Safety Analysis.

**LD** — Liquidated damages.

**Mill certs** — certified mill test reports documenting chemical and mechanical
properties of supplied steel.

**Misc metals** — items outside the structural package: lintels, ladders,
embeds, supports. The single most disputed boundary in the trade.

**Notice** — contractually required communication within a defined period.
Missing it can forfeit an otherwise valid claim, which is why it appears in
nearly every contract prompt here.

**Pay-if-paid / pay-when-paid** — payment clauses. The first makes owner payment
a condition precedent to your payment; the second addresses only timing.
Enforceability varies by state.

**Plumbing and bolting** — bringing erected steel to tolerance and completing
permanent connections.

**RFI** — Request for Information.

**Shop drawings** — fabrication and erection drawings prepared from the design
documents and submitted for review.

**Subpart R** — 29 CFR 1926 Subpart R, the OSHA standard for steel erection.

**Takeoff** — quantity survey from the drawings.

**WIP** — work in progress; percentage-of-completion accounting for active jobs.
